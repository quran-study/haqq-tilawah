# Acknowledgements

Haqq Tilawah stands on the work of others. We are grateful to every project below and follow each one's terms of use. If you build on Haqq Tilawah, please keep these credits and respect the same terms.

## Tanzil Project — Quran text

- **Website:** https://tanzil.net
- **Provides:** the verified Arabic Quran text (Uthmani script, version 1.1), the Simple Clean text (version 1.1) used for searching, and Quran metadata (surah names, ayah counts and so on).
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0).
- **Terms (from the text's copyright notice):**
  - Permission is granted to copy and distribute verbatim copies of this text, but changing it is not allowed.
  - The text can be used in any website or application, provided its source (Tanzil Project) is clearly indicated and a link is made to tanzil.net, so users can keep track of changes.
  - The copyright notice must be included in all verbatim copies and reproduced appropriately in all files derived from or containing a substantial portion of the text.
- **How we comply:** the Arabic text is used exactly as published. Its copyright notice is kept with the raw files and in `NOTICE.txt` alongside the app's data, and Tanzil is credited and linked in the app. The reader shows the Bismillah that Tanzil places at the start of ayah 1 as a heading above it, as printed mushafs do; no words are removed.

## Quranic Arabic Corpus — word-by-word morphology

- **Website:** https://corpus.quran.com
- **Provides:** the parts, grammar, root and lemma of every word (morphology version 0.4), used for the word details panel, root pages and lemma counts.
- **Copyright:** © 2011 Kais Dukes. **Licence:** GNU General Public License.
- **Terms (from the data's copyright notice):**
  - Permission is granted to copy and distribute verbatim copies of the file, but changing it is not allowed.
  - The annotation can be used in any website or application, provided its source (the Quranic Arabic Corpus) is clearly indicated and a link is made to corpus.quran.com.
  - The copyright notice must be included in all verbatim copies and reproduced appropriately in derived works.
- **How we comply:** the data file is kept unchanged, with its notice, in `data/raw/corpus`. The app reads from it, credits the Corpus wherever its data is shown, and every word panel links to that word on corpus.quran.com.
- **Note:** the Corpus was built on Tanzil's earlier text (version 1.0.2). Our build checks that every one of its 77,429 words lines up with the version 1.1 text shown in the app.
