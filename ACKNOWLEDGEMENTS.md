- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivs 3.0 (CC BY-ND 3.0).
- **Terms (from the text's copyright notice):**
  - Permission is granted to copy and distribute verbatim copies of this text, but changing it is not allowed.
  - The text can be used in any website or application, provided its source (Tanzil) is clearly indicated and a link is made to tanzil.net, so users can keep track of changes.
  - The copyright notice must be included in all verbatim copies and reproduced appropriately in derived works.
- **How we comply:** the Arabic text is used exactly as published, its copyright notice is kept with the data, and Tanzil is credited and linked in the app.

## Translations (via Tanzil)
